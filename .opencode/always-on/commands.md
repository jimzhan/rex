description: "Constitutions for OpenCode commands and workflow definitions"
---

## Ground Rules

- **Root Directory** – All `Superpowers` spec and plan files **MUST** reside under `@docs/tickets/`.
- **File Naming** – Every new spec or plan file **MUST** follow the pattern: `<id>-<slug>.<spec|plan>.md`
  - `<id>` – a unique numeric or alphanumeric identifier (e.g., `001`, `JIRA-42`) presents ticket ID.
  - `<slug>` – a short, kebab-case description of the ticket (e.g., `auth-setup`).
